How PBRMS Works — In 4 Easy Steps

A seamless ride workflow designed for daily city commuters and weekend leisure riders

1. Select Station

Choose any nearby docking station across the city and browse available bicycles by model, condition, and rate.

2. Unlock & Ride

Confirm rental instantly from your customer portal. Your ride begins with an active live timer tracking your journey.

3. Return Anywhere

Dock the bicycle at any station with available slots. The system verifies dock capacity and safely locks your bicycle.

4. Transparent Fare

Fare is calculated automatically based on elapsed duration in Indian Rupees (₹). Review your itemized digital receipt anytime.
