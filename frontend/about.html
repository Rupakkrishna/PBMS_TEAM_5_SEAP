Public Bicycle Rental Management System

Platform Overview & Service Guide

A scalable mobility platform designed to optimize public bicycle distribution, encourage green transit, and provide transparent per-hour pricing in Indian Rupees (₹).

Mission & Purpose

Urban congestion and last-mile connectivity remain major challenges for modern commuters. The Public Bicycle Rental Management System (PBRMS) provides a real-time, centralized platform connecting city commuters, bicycle fleet owners, and municipal transit administrators.

The platform guarantees data consistency through atomic database transactions, enforces strict role-based access control, and maintains an auditable simulated payment ledger for all transactions.

Platform Architecture & Security
  • Python Flask REST Architecture: Modular Blueprints for Authentication, Docking Stations, Bicycles, Rentals, Payments, and Administration.
  • Authentication & Role-Based Access: 12-round bcrypt password hashing and JWT token verification protecting user, owner, and admin portals.
  • Relational Database & ORM: Robust schema with foreign key constraints, automatic cascade checks, and atomic transaction handling.

Transparent Pricing

Indian Rupee (₹) Fare Formula

PBRMS charges riders strictly according to actual elapsed ride duration:

Total Fare (₹) = Duration (Hours) × Bicycle Hourly Rate (min 1.0 hr)
  • Minimum Billable Threshold: Any rental under 60 minutes is billed for 1 hour to prevent micro-billing anomalies (e.g. ₹15.00 min).
  • Fractional Calculation: Longer rides are calculated to two decimal places (e.g. 1 hr 30 mins at ₹20/hr = ₹30.00).
  • Simulated Ledger: Upon bike docking, a payment record is immediately recorded with status Paid (Simulated).
Affordable City Commutes

Rates start from just ₹15 / hour for standard city cycles up to ₹40 / hour for electric-assist bicycles.
